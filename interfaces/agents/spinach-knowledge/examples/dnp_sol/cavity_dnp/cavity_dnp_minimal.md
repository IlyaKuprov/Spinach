# cavity_dnp_minimal

`result=cavity_dnp_minimal(nlevels,max_rank,ncycles)` simulates the full low-field E/209Bi/Cn donor-cavity system exclusively in `sphten-liouv`. The optional Fokker–Planck pulse rank and saturation-sweep count default to 2 and 300. It illustrates Fig. 1 and Appendix D of Lorriaux et al., arXiv:2610.19180v1, without an ensemble fit or a replacement nuclear spin.

Native `equilibrium` prepares the donor at 25 mK. Native `reduce` removes a disconnected cavity spectator from pulse calculations without reducing the twenty donor levels or changing formalism. Eigenvalues only of the native left-product Liouville Hamiltonian determine transition frequencies. Native finite `shaped_pulse_af` pulses saturate the eight ground-manifold lines, perform microwave shelving, and perform the RF population transfer. Native `coherence` implements uniform phase cycling of total donor projection; it preserves the zero-quantum electron–nuclear coherences required at low field.

Native `BL1` attaches vacuum. The connected Hamiltonian is rebuilt from `inter.coupling.scalar` and `inter.modes` specifications; native relaxation and multichannel evolution perform the laboratory-frame reset. All Zeeman energies and the full isotropic hyperfine interaction are retained. Returned measurements are nuclear Iz, photon occupation, trace, Fock-edge occupation, and preparation convergence diagnostics, together with native objects for independent controls.

The example uses one identity `state(s,'E',1)`, not the summed identity `state(s,'E','all')`. Physical observable duals use native left-product operators. Approximations include disconnected cavity during pulses, finite rectangular pulses instead of WURST shapes, zero-temperature photon bath, illustrative 100 Hz single-donor coupling, and omission of intrinsic relaxation and direct nuclear magnetic drive.
