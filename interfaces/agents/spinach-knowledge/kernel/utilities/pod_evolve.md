# Range-aware evaluation of a fixed POD family

`pod_evolve(model,coefficients,timestep,nsteps)` returns the initial complex observable and a uniform trajectory of the prepared affine model. Inputs and integer counts are double; nsteps is nonnegative and below flintmax. Terms use L=H+1i*R+1i*K in rad/s, time is in seconds, and the sign is exp(-1i*L*t).

Time, coefficients and active terms are decomposed into bounded components and binary exponents. Assembly uses one common exponent frame and restores only the summed generator, preserving finite cancellation without trial multiplication orders or fallback chains. Lost nonzero components and an unrepresentable final generator are explicit errors. Zero coefficients are excluded. Sparse terms remain sparse until a dense reduced exponential is actually needed.

Initial coordinates and the detector are normalised separately. The propagator factors out a representable real scalar growth, is binary normalised, and updates a binary-normalised working state. Physical amplitude is carried separately; only the detected Hermitian overlap is restored. A finite observable can therefore remain supported beyond the physical state's double range. A shift whose exponent is outside exact integer range is not used. Nonfinite shifted propagators, unsupported working-array dynamic range, inexactly representable accumulated exponents and nonfinite final observables are explicit errors. This is not arbitrary-precision or componentwise-error certification for ill-conditioned dynamics.

Zero-step, zero-duration, all-zero coefficients, zero initial state/detector and exact common-side structural stationary support return constant traces after input validation. A state-side shortcut must hold for every active term, or a detector-side shortcut must hold for every active term; different sides do not combine. The assembled support is also checked. Computed actions are never used to infer zeros because they can underflow. Cancellation-based nullspaces otherwise use the general exponential; zero initial overlap does not imply a constant signal.

One MATLAB expm propagator is amortised over the steps of the small reduced model. The maintained scaling/squaring approach is described by Al-Mohy and Higham, DOI:10.1137/09074721X. Their exponential-action alternative, DOI:10.1137/100788860, is appropriate for large matrix actions but is not selected through an undocumented size heuristic here. This evaluator does not train, retune rank, change a physical spin_system, infer a metric, subtract references or process spectra. No positivity, trace, stability or observable-error guarantee follows from snapshot energy or orthogonality.

Hermitian detection accumulates real component products by their binary exponents before restoring signal units. Structural directed reachability retains exactly the coordinates on paths from the state to the detector, excluding unrelated unstable blocks. Normalized matrix products explicitly reject unsupported component underflow.

The matrix-product range contract conservatively requires every nonzero operand-component pair to remain nonzero at the working scale; it can reject a physically finite result rather than silently lose a contribution.
