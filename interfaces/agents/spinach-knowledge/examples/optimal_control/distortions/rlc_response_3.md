- Signature: `rlc_response_3()`
- Calculation time: the source header estimates minutes.

## Purpose and model

This is the piecewise-linear counterpart to `rlc_response_2`: it models the effect of a probe-circuit response on a simulated deuterium pre-phasing pulse for the CD3 group of alanine. The source states that the desired magnetisation is set for rephasing 100 microseconds after the pulse. The model contains 2H with the alanine-CD3 NQI input `anas2mat(0,40e3,0,0,0,0)`; the magnet is set to 14.1 T (described in the source as 600 MHz). The calculation uses the full sphten-liouv basis and the 100-orientation powder grid `rep_2ang_100pts_sph`, with lab-frame powder drift Liouvillians from `drifts`.

## Pulse design and response

The normalised transition is 2H `Lz` to `Lx`, controlled by the 2H `Lx` and `Ly` operators on one channel. Five RF levels—40 to 60 kHz in 5-kHz steps—are used for robustness. The pulse has 75 time intervals of 2 microseconds each (150 microseconds total) and a 100-microsecond dead-time setting. The piecewise-linear control has 76 node samples for those 75 intervals; the first and last four nodes are frozen. The source sets LBFGS with a maximum of 100 iterations and an NS penalty of weight 1, and calls `fmaxnewton(spin_system,@grape_xy,guess)` from `randn(2,76)/10` after zeroing the first and last four samples. Optimiser plots are requested for XY controls, robustness, and the spectrogram.

The Cartesian controls are scaled by the mean RF power and sent to `restrans` at the 2H Larmor frequency, with Q = 200 and the `pwl_tsc` response option (plus the source's final argument 100). Unlike `rlc_response_2`, this script does not assign `control.integrator`. The plotted response is generated by this model call; no measured pulse or reported quantitative outcome is included. It therefore documents a simulated comparison, not hardware validation or an established performance defect.
