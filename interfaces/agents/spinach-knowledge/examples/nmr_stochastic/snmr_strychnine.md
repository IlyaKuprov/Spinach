# examples/nmr_stochastic/snmr_strychnine.m

- Signature: `snmr_strychnine()`

## Purpose

Runs a Primas-style stochastic NMR trajectory for strychnine. The source states that an NVIDIA Titan V GPU is required at minimum and estimates minutes. Author: Ilya Kuprov (ilya.kuprov@weizmann.ac.il).

## Model and calculation

The 1H spin system is generated by the strychnine helper and simulated at 5.9 T. The basis uses IK-2 with scalar-coupling connectivity and proximity level 1; the proximity cutoff is 4.0. Redfield relaxation uses IME equilibrium, 298 K, kite retention, and tau_c=200e-12 s.

The script constructs the Hamiltonian and relaxation superoperator, then generates two independent uniform-noise controls over 10000 steps, bounded by omega_max=1e2 rad/s. With dt=1e-5 s, each step forms the generator from the Hamiltonian, relaxation, and x/y controls and advances the thermal-equilibrium state. GPU arrays hold the operators and trajectory; the GPU enable option in the source is commented out.

## Output

After transferring the trajectory back from the GPU, the example calculates the 1H L+ signal and plots the control sequence and complex signal trajectory.
