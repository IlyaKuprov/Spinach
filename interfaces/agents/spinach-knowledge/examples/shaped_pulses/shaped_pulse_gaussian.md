# examples/shaped_pulses/shaped_pulse_gaussian.m

- Signature: `shaped_pulse_gaussian()`

## Purpose

This example applies a sampled Gaussian pulse labelled as a 90-degree pulse to the Lz state of a 31-proton chain. It then simulates liquid-state acquisition and plots the imaginary part of the processed spectrum. The plotted spectrum is generated by the model; the source contains no measured result or convergence validation.

## Spin system and waveform

The chain is at 14.1 T, with scalar Zeeman shifts from −4 to +4 ppm and 10 Hz scalar couplings between neighbouring protons. The script uses an IK-2 basis with scalar-coupling connectivity, proximity level 1, and the NMR assumption.

The waveform is read from gaussian_1000.pk as amplitudes and phases, sampled at 80 points over 0.015 s. Each interval is 0.015/80 s (187.5 μs). The samples are scaled by the source's calibration factor, (π/2) × 80 divided by (sum of amplitudes × 0.015 s), then converted from amplitude/phase to Cartesian Cx/Cy components. The pulse is applied with a 480 Hz frequency offset through H + 2π × 480 × Lz and shaped_pulse_xy's expv-pwc piecewise-constant propagation.

## Acquisition and observable

The initial state is 1H Lz; Lx/Ly are the RF controls and Lz supplies the offset term. The pulse-prepared state is acquired with a 5000 Hz sweep, 2048 points, zero filling to 16384 points, and a Hz axis. Exponential apodisation uses parameter 6; the imaginary part of the Fourier-transformed FID is plotted. The script has no explicit spatial-gradient or homospoil stage and does not construct a relaxation superoperator.

The shaped_pulse_xy implementation cites DOI [10.1016/j.jmr.2004.08.017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmr.2004.08.017). See [shaped_pulse_gaussian.m](https://github.com/IlyaKuprov/Spinach/blob/main/examples/shaped_pulses/shaped_pulse_gaussian.m) and [shaped_pulse_xy.m](https://github.com/IlyaKuprov/Spinach/blob/main/kernel/pulses/shaped_pulse_xy.m).
