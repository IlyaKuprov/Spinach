# Literature-sourced spin table

`isotopes.tsv` is the editable source; `build_isotopes(source_file)` generates the typed, uncompressed `isotopes.mat` release payload. Normal `spin` calls do not read text, download data, or regenerate files. Numerical gamma is signed angular frequency per field, in rad/(s*T). Magnetic moments are in nuclear magnetons, quadrupole moments in barns, abundances are fractions, and half-lives are seconds. Nuclear g factors are magnetic moment divided by nonzero spin; particle g factors are not defined by that nuclear convention.

## Coverage and state identity

The minimum selection is every experimentally established isotope with half-life strictly above one second, including stable and spin-zero nuclei, and qualifying metastable states. The isotope threshold does not apply to magnetic particles. The adopted inventory is NUBASE2020's recorded discoveries, supplemented by cited subsequent observational papers and a current ENSDF/NuDat long-lived-state inventory check. Nuclear states with an adopted half-life below one second are excluded when the lifetime qualification is a value, estimate, or upper limit. Exactly one second, stable nuclei, unknown lifetimes, and unresolved lower limits are retained: a lower bound below one second does not establish a subsecond lifetime. The cutoff uses the adopted state/environment value, not alternative lifetimes in notes; consequently, the neutral-atom `229Th_m` row is excluded despite its longer crystal radiative lifetimes. Unobserved mass predictions are not physical rows. Superseded state assignments are replaced consistently, not appended as duplicate discoveries.

Coverage is an evaluated literature inventory, not a proof that every unpublished result or subsequently unindexed isomer is represented. The literature check includes discovery papers through 2026-10-03 and the ENSDF distribution of 2026-10-01. Evaluated lists lag some primary papers: the cited 2025 Ag/Rh state assignments and the 2021 high-K `251Md_m` observation are explicit supplements. Unknown spins, signed moments, absolute isomer energies, and lifetimes remain unknown. A distinct evaluated `152Pm_x` activity is retained with unresolved state identity; questioned, unconfirmed Cu/Nb activities remain notes, not invented established states.

Suffixes identify explicit states, not an assumed universal excitation ordering. `180Ta` is the spin-one, approximately eight-hour ground state; natural tantalum-180 is `180Ta_m`, the spin-nine isomer. The 2025 `118Ag` ordering is ground spin four, `_m` spin zero, and `_n` spin seven. State matching uses evaluated energy intervals and compatible spins, or an explicitly audited spin/lifetime identity; it never forces an old excited-state moment into a ground-state row. Unmatched moment records are not guessed into the table.

## Adopted academic sources

- **Spin and half-life:** Kondev et al., *Chinese Physics C* **45**, 030001 (2021), NUBASE2020, DOI **10.1088/1674-1137/abddae**, and its fixed-width companion table. Original J/parity flags, lifetime uncertainties/limits, excitation qualifiers, and discovery information are retained. A NUBASE year is 31,556,926 seconds. Later adopted measurements have their own field-labelled DOI and source qualification, including **10.1103/PhysRevC.106.064306**, **10.1103/PhysRevC.108.024611**, **10.1016/j.physletb.2026.140365**, **10.1103/PhysRevC.111.014329**, **10.1016/j.physletb.2025.139359**, and **10.1140/epja/s10050-021-00631-4**. Observations without measured lifetimes do not receive predicted numerical substitutes.
- **Magnetic and quadrupole moments:** Stone, *Atomic Data and Nuclear Data Tables* **90**, 75–176 (2005), DOI **10.1016/j.adt.2005.04.001**; Pyykkö's stated standard quadrupole set, *Molecular Physics* **116**, 1328–1338 (2018), DOI **10.1080/00268976.2018.1426131**. Explicit later preferred magnetic values come from Makulski's review, DOI **10.3390/magnetochemistry12010012**, with the `11B` value/spin sourced to **10.3390/magnetochemistry9060148**. Signed Cu moments are supported by **10.1103/PhysRevC.82.064311**; `11Be` ground-state spin/moment by **10.1103/PhysRevLett.83.3792**. The angular ratio used for `103Rh` is explicitly reported in Holmes et al., *Chemical Science* **15**, 2181–2196 (2024), DOI **10.1039/d3sc06026h**; its rounded NMR convention and conversion to moment are qualified in the row, rather than silently changing the conflicting older printed scalar. The inaccessible Stone2016 table was not used under its DOI.
- **Natural compositions:** Meija et al., *Pure and Applied Chemistry* **88**, 293–306 (2016), 2013 evaluation, DOI **10.1515/pac-2015-0503**. This is an explicitly declared baseline, not a claim to incorporate all later CIAAW recommendations. Representative numbers and published terrestrial ranges are separate. Interval-only recommendations have scalar abundance NaN; no midpoint or particular-sample value is substituted. Parenthetic composition uncertainties are not relabelled standard uncertainties. Missing tabulated composition is unknown, not zero; radioactivity does not imply zero natural abundance.
- **Constants and free particles:** CODATA2022, *Reviews of Modern Physics* **97**, 025002 (2025), DOI **10.1103/RevModPhys.97.025002**; PDG2024, *Physical Review D* **110**, 030001, DOI **10.1103/PhysRevD.110.030001**; antiproton moment, Smorra et al., *Nature* **550**, 371–374 (2017), DOI **10.1038/nature24048**. The antineutron sign convention is also supported by **10.1016/j.physrep.2015.11.001**.

Stone's table order is not a recommendation. Outside explicit preferred/standard sets, the adoption rule selects the smallest reported absolute uncertainty among signed direct AB/D candidates when available, then explicitly diamagnetism-corrected candidates, then other signed scalar candidates. This is not a claim of the latest or universally recommended value. The adopted raw value, qualification, uncertainty, incompatible candidates, and correction-convention alternatives remain in notes. Values without uncertainties are not presumed more precise; incompatible intervals are not averaged. An unsigned or tentative sign is not turned into a positive default. In particular, `139Ce`, `209Po`, `231Pa`, and `247Cm` lack an adopted signed gamma; `180Ta` ground state lacks an adopted moment.

## Physical qualifications

The nuclear conversion is `gamma=(mu/I)*mu_N/hbar`, using CODATA2022 `mu_N=5.0507837393e-27 J/T` and exact `h=6.62607015e-34 J*s`, with `hbar=h/(2*pi)`. Direct source particle gamma is retained; moment-derived hyperon gamma follows `mu/(hbar*J)`, not a nuclear g-factor assignment. In particular, `Omega-` has J=3/2, not 1/2; its unmeasured spectroscopic quadrupole moment and that of its counterpart remain NaN. Spin zero has exact magnetic moment/gamma zero; spin below one has no spectroscopic quadrupole moment. For higher spin, missing Q is NaN rather than zero. Numerical calls require confirmed spin and signed gamma; tentative or estimated rows are still inspectable as metadata.

Particle keys are `E`, `E+`, `N`, `antiN`, `M`, `M+`, `anti1H`, the six static-moment hyperons `Lambda`, `Sigma+`, `Sigma-`, `Xi0`, `Xi-`, `Omega-`, and their `anti`-prefixed counterparts. `1H` remains the single proton row. Counterpart properties inferred under CPT are explicitly labelled, not independently measured. Sigma-zero's transition moment is not a static Zeeman moment; tau bounds are not measured moments. Short particle lifetimes do not prevent inclusion.

Free-particle half-life is `ln(2)*mean_lifetime`; source mean life, uncertainty, and conversion remain explicit. Muon values exclude capture in matter. Stable Inf means classified stable/no observed intrinsic decay, not experimental proof of infinite lifetime; decay limits and annihilation are not confused with an intrinsic half-life. Neutral `229Th_m` is excluded by its microsecond internal-conversion half-life. Longer measured radiative lifetimes in MgF2/CaF2 do not change that adopted neutral-atom cutoff; the environmental distinction is documented by DOIs **10.1038/s41586-023-05894-z** and **10.1103/PhysRevLett.132.182501**. A neutral/hydrogen-like `186Ta` study is qualified as an unresolved state-identity/lifetime alternative, DOI **10.1103/PhysRevC.104.024330**.

## Updating and validating

Audit the academic property and state source before editing the TSV, rebuild the MAT file, and run `kernel/spin_table` and relevant importer/integration regressions. Check source/payload equality, original qualifications, mandatory coverage, and unit/sign invariants. Compare against EasySpin only after literature adoption; EasySpin is a cross-check, not a numerical authority for this table. Different free/shielded moment, abundance, and isomer conventions require explanation rather than an automatic overwrite.

Each process loads the release payload once and caches a label dictionary and numeric projections. Warm numeric calls have no filesystem operations or metadata slicing. Clear `spin` or start a new process after a payload update. Missing payloads fail rather than invoking an online or TSV fallback; metadata does not automatically alter abundance weighting, decay, or existing propagation algorithms.
