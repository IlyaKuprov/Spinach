# ddf_sphere_decay: reflecting radial diffusion

Compare the voxel and analytic amplitudes of the second nonconstant radial Neumann mode on a sphere. The diffusion coefficient is 0.001 m²/s and the longitudinal lifetime is 5 s. The full final-state error detects boundary distortions hidden by a scalar projection. For the archived cases in Bouchard, JCP 164, 144121, use (count,radius)=(10,0.25) and (12,0.30); increasing the first argument refines the mask at fixed geometry. The sphere tables contain transport and relaxation without dipolar feedback, not a measurement of the regularised field.
