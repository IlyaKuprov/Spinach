# examples/esr_sol_swept/qband_orisel_demo.m

Demonstrates rigid MTSSL-14N orientation selection at exactly 34 GHz and 1.2097, 1.2112, 1.2162 T. Uses aligned illustrative g=[2.0090 2.0060 2.0020] and A=[14 14 95] MHz tensors, 20 K, a 100 MHz Gaussian intensity FWHM, and an 800-point full-sphere grid. Three visible-axis sphere plots show independently peak-normalized selection; titles report retained grid sizes at a 1% integrated cutoff.

This is a CPU-only, no-argument teaching example, not a fitted spectrum, a field-sweep engine, or a pulse simulation. Increase the quadrature to check convergence for quantitative work. Strain, relaxation, saturation, motion, other spin labels, and external ENDOR dependencies are absent.
