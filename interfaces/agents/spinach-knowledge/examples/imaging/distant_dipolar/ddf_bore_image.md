# Isolated water cylinder with an eccentric axial bore

`ddf_bore_image(npts,steps)` returns complex Cartesian readouts for two finite-lobe preparations, with identical instantaneous XY encoding after 30 ms. The single-species Bloch reduction includes dipolar feedback, relaxation and reflecting diffusion. Preparation is along Z for the first shot and along `(X+Z)/sqrt(2)` for the second. Both images use one single-pulse reference. The bore has no spin density but can experience a nonzero dipolar field and finite-resolution reconstruction sidelobes. Compare signed real and imaginary central profiles at fixed readout bandwidth. To measure relative spatial error without changes in the voxelised single-pulse reference affecting the comparison, keep the time steps fixed and compare the unscaled complex k-space. To measure temporal sensitivity, halve the step sizes at fixed spatial grid, including during the finite lobes. The reduced-grid direct-space oracle and decreasing spatial changes do not certify convergence to the curved continuum phantom. This is a synthetic phantom, not a reproduction of measured water images.
