# examples/nmr_solids/das_two_angle.m

Carries each crystallite and its rotor phase through a lab-frame axis jump, holding the spin state and receiver in the laboratory frame. Checks equal-axis composition and compares the changed-angle trace with explicit Cartesian midpoint evolution.

The example uses a rank-17 three-angle quadrature. For the displayed time grid, refining to rank 23 changes the two complex coherences by at most 1.1e-9.
