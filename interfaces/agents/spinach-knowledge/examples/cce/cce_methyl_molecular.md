# cce_methyl_molecular

`cce_methyl_molecular()` plots the **36-proton APPA** curve in Fig. 7 of [Jeschke et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.5194/mr-6-15-2025), for the twelve methyl groups of the Finland trityl core. It does not calculate the other simulated traces in the panel. The comparison experiment is the archived bare-electron Hahn echo of FTR 1 in deuterated o-terphenyl, normalised and scaled by 0.981 as in the authors' plotting script.

The calculation is the analytical high-field limit of projected CCE-2: the proton Zeeman term and the pair Ising term commute with the isolated pair evolution, the one-proton factors are unity, and Eq. (9) supplies a factor for each of the 630 proton pairs. Intramethyl tunnelling contributes a coherent exchange term (Eq. 8), not a stochastic jump rate. The powder average uses the stock `icos_2ang_642pts` grid, selected by name through `parameters.grid`, and averages the completed cluster products.

Proton coordinates and Cartesian hyperfine tensors enter stock `create` with `sys.tols.prox_cutoff=0`, so no dipolar network is built; `clexpn(@hahn_echo)` with `cce.backend='analytical_hahn'` takes the proton point dipoles from the coordinates and applies the projected secular pair limit at each quadrature node. The simulation evaluates the real, normalised echo on a 2048-point grid from zero to 40 microseconds.

The labelled proton and echo CSV files reproduce the archived DFT tensors, geometry, and experimental trace (G. Jeschke et al., [Zenodo 13850793](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13850793), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). The two tunnel splittings are specified directly in the example. No archived simulation output is needed to run the example. The original three-proton disjoint-CCE illustration is retained as a dense-reference regression fixture.
