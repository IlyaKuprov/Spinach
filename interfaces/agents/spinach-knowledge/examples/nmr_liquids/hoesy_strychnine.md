# examples/nmr_liquids/hoesy_strychnine.m

- Signature: `hoesy_strychnine()`

## Purpose

13C{1H} HOESY spectrum of strychnine with natural content of 13C isotope. Calculation time: minutes

## Physical / mathematical content

The example simulates a 13C-detected, 1H-decoupled HOESY spectrum of strychnine. The spin system is supplied by `strychnine({'1H','13C'})`; the calculation includes Redfield relaxation and averages over the 13C isotopomers generated by dilution.

## Numerical / algorithmic content

It uses an IK-1 sphten-liouv basis with scalar-coupling connectivity, proximity level 3 and interaction level 4. The 0.5 s mixing-time signal is square-cosine apodised and Fourier transformed in both dimensions; isotopomer simulations are accumulated in a `parfor` loop.

## Implementation structure

The field is 14.1 T; Redfield parameters are correlation time 50e-12 s and temperature 298 K. Acquisition uses sweeps [6000 18000] Hz, offsets [3000 12000] Hz, 128 points per dimension and 512-point zero filling. The final plot uses positive display polarity.
