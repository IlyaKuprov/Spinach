# examples/nmr_paramag/calbindin/tm_1igv_mult_fit.m

- MATLAB implementation: [examples/nmr_paramag/calbindin/tm_1igv_mult_fit.m](https://github.com/IlyaKuprov/Spinach/blob/main/examples/nmr_paramag/calbindin/tm_1igv_mult_fit.m)

- Signature: `tm_1igv_mult_fit()`

## Purpose and data

This example uses a point-electron model to recover an electron location and susceptibility tensor from experimental pseudocontact shifts (PCS). The source credits the data to Gottfried Otting (Australian National University). It loads `expt_pcs` and the corresponding `x`, `y`, and `z` positions from `tm_1igv_pcs.mat`; unlike the distribution-reconstruction scripts, it does not load the processed 1IGV PDB or use PDB atom coordinates. No nuclear-isotope list, coordinate unit, or tensor unit is stated in this file.

## Fit and plotted output

The inverse calculation is the source call `ilpcs([x y z],expt_pcs,[0 1 2],[-5 5 -15])`. Its returned values are assigned to `mxyz` (point-electron location), `chi` (susceptibility tensor), and `pred_pcs` (predicted PCS); the third output is discarded. The script plots experimental against predicted PCS as blue circles and adds the identity line. Both axes are labelled in ppm and limited to the combined minimum and maximum of the measured and predicted values. It then displays `chi` and `mxyz`.

The plot is a diagnostic comparison generated by the example; this source alone does not establish numerical fit quality or experimental reproduction. It does not save the fitted outputs.
