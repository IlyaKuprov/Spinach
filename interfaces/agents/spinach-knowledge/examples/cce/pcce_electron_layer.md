# Partitioned Hahn echo in a two-dimensional electron lattice

`[answer,diagnostics]=pcce_electron_layer()` reconstructs the blue pCCE(2,1), green internally averaged pCCE(2,2), and orange normally averaged pCCE(2,2) series in [Schätzle et al., supplementary Fig. S4(a)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16169). Columns of the result are the three normalised NV magnetisations. Diagnostics supplies total echo times in seconds, lattice sites in nm, and the dimer partition.

The two NV branches have magnetic quantum numbers 0 and −1. All 20 bath electrons interact by secular dipolar couplings. Within each cluster both echo paths are propagated and traced at infinite temperature; outside it, a common random longitudinal spin configuration supplies the static field. For singleton partitions, the example averages after forming the second-order cluster product; for dimers, it evaluates both averaging orders. The distinction matters when a cluster response appears in a denominator.

The supplement specifies a planar lattice with 20 nm nearest-neighbour spacing, but not the precise sites, partition, or exterior spin draws. The example uses the 20 nearest square-lattice sites around the NV, a minimum-total-squared-distance pairing, and 100 reproducible exterior configurations. These are reconstruction choices, not original simulation inputs. It does not compute the exact 20-spin benchmark or the size-four and size-five partition curves.

Comparison with identifiable published marker centres gives RMS magnetisation differences of 0.025 (10 blue markers) and 0.041 (16 green markers). The normally averaged dimer result is not a quantitative match: it first exceeds unity at 200 μs on this grid, while the published orange series is still below 0.15 there. The plotted ordinate is limited to the published range; the returned values are not clipped.
