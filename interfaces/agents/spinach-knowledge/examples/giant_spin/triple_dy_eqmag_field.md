# examples/giant_spin/triple_dy_eqmag_field.m

- Signature: `triple_dy_eqmag_field()`

## Purpose

Simulation of the field dependence of the magnetisation of a triple- Dy triangular complex -see Figure S27 and S28 in the Supplementary Information of the following paper Ligand field parameters and g-tensor for the J=15/2 ground term were computed using the SINGLE_ANISO routine in MOLCAS. Calculation time: hours

https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.201703842

## Physical / mathematical content

The model contains three J=15/2 dysprosium centres in a triangular arrangement. It uses anisotropic g-tensors, ligand-field terms of ranks 2, 4 and 6, an exchange coupling of 0.0063 cm⁻¹ between each pair, and spin-orbit corrections to the dipole–dipole couplings. The supplied dysprosium coordinates define the triangle. The g-tensors at the other two sites are generated by successive 120° rotations of the third site's tensor.

The Stevens ligand-field coefficients are converted from cm⁻¹ to Hz, converted to irreducible spherical tensors and rotated into the molecular frame. Site-dependent Euler angles orient the ligand fields around the triangle.

## Numerical / algorithmic content

The calculation uses the `zeeman-hilb` basis without approximation, a `leb_2ang_rank_11` spherical powder grid and a temperature of 2.0 K. At each specified magnetic field from 0.01 to 6 T, it creates the spin system, calls `eqmag` and records the Z component of the equilibrium magnetisation.

## Output and comparison

The script plots calculated magnetisation against magnetic field in Bohr magnetons. On each field step, it also loads `field` and `magn` from `triple_dy_eqmag.mat` and plots those experimental values as points.
