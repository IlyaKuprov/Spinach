# examples/optimal_control/features_ampl.m

- Signature: `features_ampl()`
- Calculation time: the source header estimates minutes.

## Purpose and spin model

This example fixes an amplitude envelope and optimises the phase of a pulse to excite a selected interval of a simulated 13C offset ensemble. The model is 100 non-interacting spins at equally spaced offsets from −160 to +160 ppm, at 14.1 T. It uses the sphten-liouv formalism with the IK-2 approximation and proximal level 1. The normalised initial and target states are `Lz` and `Lx` for spins 21–80; the 20 spins at each end are not included in that target definition.

## Pulse optimisation

The two controls are the 13C `Lx` and `Ly` operators on one channel, with the drift Hamiltonian generated by `hamiltonian(assume(spin_system,'nmr'))`. The prescribed, dimensionless amplitude envelope is `exp(-linspace(-2,2,250).^2)`; 250 slices of 2 microseconds give a 500-microsecond waveform. RF power levels span 15–20 kHz in 11 steps (stored as angular-frequency values, `2*pi*linspace(15e3,20e3,11)`). The phase starts from a constant pi/6 guess. The RF scale is the mean of the 11 power levels multiplied by the prescribed envelope. The source sets LBFGS, a maximum of 1000 iterations, and a DNS smoothness penalty with weight 1, and calls `fmaxnewton(spin_system,@grape_phase,guess)`. The optimised phase is combined with the fixed envelope using `polar2cartesian`; the source also requests control, phase, robustness, and spectrogram plots.

## Simulated signal and output

The resulting pulse is propagated with `shaped_pulse_xy(...,'expv-pwc')` from a 13C `Lz` state. The resulting state is used for a simulated liquid-state NMR FID with the 13C `L+` coil, zero offset, a 55,000-Hz sweep, 2,048 acquired points, and zero filling to 16,384 points. The axis is inverted and labelled in ppm; the FID is Gaussian-apodised with the source parameter 10, Fourier transformed, and plotted with reference lines at −96 and +96 ppm. The script specifies a design and simulated spectrum but supplies no reported excitation profile or numerical optimisation result, so no achieved performance should be inferred from the example alone.
