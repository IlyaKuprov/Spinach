SpinXML schema maintenance

spinxml_schema_total.xsd describes the complete format. The two partial schemas,
spinxml_schema.xsd and spinxml_schema_reference_frame.xsd, are used to generate
the typed DataSet headers in the parent directory.

After changing a schema, regenerate its header in Visual Studio. Compare with
the previous header and retain local changes not produced by the generator.
Do not leave the XSD as an automatic project input: rebuilding would overwrite
those changes.
