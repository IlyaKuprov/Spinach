## Purpose

Digital processing for a recording of the proton optimal control pulse, done on a 1 GHz oscilloscope using the 13C coil of the same probe: the pulse is heterodyned down to the rotating frame and compared with the ideal version.

## Physical / mathematical content

- Optimal-control examples. These scripts formulate pulse design as a nonlinear optimisation problem over waveform samples or basis coefficients. The core mathematical objects are fidelities, gradients, Hessians or Hessian approximations, ensemble robustness objectives, and constrained search over RF amplitude/phase trajectories.
