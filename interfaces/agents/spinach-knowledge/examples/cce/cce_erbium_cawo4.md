# cce_erbium_cawo4

`cce_erbium_cawo4()` plots the ensemble mean Hahn echo amplitudes of the lattice and amorphous baths in Fig. S12 of [Le Dantec et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abj9786).

The model is a secular, mixed-state (^{183}\mathrm{W}) bath within 11 nm of an Er ion at a Ca site. The field lies at 46.5° in the crystal plane. Only its direction enters the echo: the identical nuclear Zeeman terms commute with the secular pair Hamiltonians and cancel under Hahn refocusing. With no transverse hyperfine terms, a single nucleus produces no echo modulation. Each two-nucleus cluster is evaluated analytically in its flip-flop subspace, so the material calculation does not need to instantiate millions of Spinach Hamiltonians. The absolute effect of pruning is bounded by the sum of the omitted pairs' maximum possible echo losses.

The lattice geometry and occupancy follow the [authors' Dataverse archive](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/9VSAUT); the dipolar prefactors use the article's SI Hamiltonian rather than the rounded numerical prefactors in the archived one-realisation driver. The archive contains the published curves and a one-realisation Fortran driver, but not its external position input files or the original random draws. The example uses 50 independently generated isotope configurations and 50 uniform spatial configurations with the archived continuum spin count. The amorphous sampling in the archived driver sets no minimum distance, which lets nuclei overlap; the example adds continuum sites one at a time and rejects any closer than 1.12 Å to an accepted site, twice the Shannon crystal radius of four-coordinate W(6+). It reconstructs the model, not the original stochastic trajectories. Each realisation is a separate Spinach system created with `sys.tols.prox_cutoff=0`, so stock `create` keeps the coordinates without building a dipolar network; projected `clexpn(@hahn_echo)` with `cce.backend='analytical_hahn'` takes the point dipoles from those coordinates. The lattice and amorphous realisations are stacked and evaluated in one `parfor` loop, each iteration building its own system from a local copy of `sys` and calling `create` and `clexpn` in place. Neither system creation nor the pair expansion allocates a dense bath interaction table.

`cce_erbium_cawo4_small()` retains the original illustrative three-spin `clexpn` example with schematic couplings; it is not a quantitative model of the material.
