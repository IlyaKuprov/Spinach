# tests/kernel/test_clexpn_transition.m

[MATLAB source](https://github.com/IlyaKuprov/Spinach/blob/main/tests/kernel/test_clexpn_transition.m)

Registered as kernel/clexpn_transition. Checks exact spin-one m=0,+1 vectors, unlike-spin tensor ordering, analytic native angular-frequency gaps, deterministic ascending-energy selection, refusal of unresolved zero-field degeneracy, acceptance of explicit orthonormal states, and rejection of unknown, mixed, invalid, or mismatched selectors. Frequencies are checked against the native isotope gyromagnetic ratios rather than an old CCE entry point.
