# examples/dnp_mas/cross_effect_mas_dynam.m

- MATLAB implementation: [examples/dnp_mas/cross_effect_mas_dynam.m](https://github.com/IlyaKuprov/Spinach/blob/main/examples/dnp_mas/cross_effect_mas_dynam.m)

- Signature: `cross_effect_mas_dynam()`
- Calculation time: seconds
- [MATLAB source](../../../../../examples/dnp_mas/cross_effect_mas_dynam.m)
- Reference: [Mentink-Vigier et al., Journal of Magnetic Resonance (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmr.2015.07.001)

## Purpose

Builds a magic-angle-spinning cross-effect DNP trajectory for a single crystal over the first rotor period, following the approach in the cited paper. The source explicitly cautions that Spinach's rotation conventions differ from the paper's.

## Spin system and relaxation

The system contains two electrons and one proton at `sys.magnet=9.394`. Both electron Zeeman tensors have eigenvalues [2.0094, 2.0060, 2.0017]; the first Euler triple is zero and the second is `pi*[107,108,124]/180`. The proton Zeeman tensor is zero. The electron–electron coupling tensor is [23.0e6, −11.5e6, −11.5e6] with Euler angles `pi*[0,135,0]/180`; the first electron–proton tensor is [1.5e6, −0.75e6, −0.75e6] with zero Euler angles.

Relaxation uses `nottingham`, with rates assigned as `nott_r1e=1/0.3e-3`, `nott_r1n=1/4.0`, `nott_r2e=1/1.0e-6`, and `nott_r2n=1/0.2e-3`. Temperature is 100, equilibrium is Di Bari, and relaxation retention is secular. The basis is `sphten-liouv` with no approximation.

## Rotor stack and propagation

The stack is generated by `rotor_stack(spin_system,parameters,'esr')`. Its settings are axis [sqrt(2/3), 0, sqrt(1/3)], no additional rotating frames, orientation `pi*[320,141,80]/180`, spins `{'E','1H'}`, magnet-frame MAS, offset [0,0], and maximum rank 3000. The electron microwave operator is `Lx`; the electron offset operator is `Lz`. Experiment parameters are `mw_pwr=0.85e6`, `mw_off=-400e6`, and `rate=12.5e3`.

After computing `R=relaxation(spin_system)`, the script obtains thermal equilibrium from `equilibrium(spin_system,hamiltonian(assume(spin_system,'labframe'),'left'))`. It allocates a complex density-operator trajectory for `numel(H)` stack points. For each subsequent point it calls `step` with the stack Hamiltonian plus the 2π-scaled microwave and electron-offset operators and `1i*R`; the step duration is `1/(nsteps*rate)`.

## Output and scope

`trajan(spin_system,rho,'level_populations')` analyses the trajectory at the level-population level. The calculation represents a single-crystal trajectory over one rotor period; it does not average over orientations or report a steady state.
