# examples/relaxation_theory/sle_esr_nitroxide_1.m

- Signature: `sle_esr_nitroxide_1()`

## Purpose

Compares an ESR spectrum of a nitroxide calculated with stochastic Liouville equation (SLE) dynamics against the spectrum calculated with Redfield relaxation theory. Calculation time: seconds.

## Physical / mathematical content

The spin system is loaded from `../standard_systems/nitroxide.log` as two electrons and one `14N`, at `3.5 T`, with a proximity cutoff of `4.0`. Both calculations use a correlation time of `5e-11 s`; the SLE uses maximum rank `10`, while the Redfield setup retains secular terms and sets the equilibrium state to zero.

## Numerical / algorithmic content

The complete `sphten-liouv` basis is used without approximation. For both spectra the initial and detection states are electron `L+`, and the frequency sweep is `[-3e8,-1e8]` with `1024` points and `1024` zero-fill points in `GHz-labframe` units. The SLE spectrum is generated by `gridfree` with `slowpass`; the Redfield spectrum by `liquid` with the same acquisition parameters. Each result is processed with `fdvec(...,5,1)` and the two spectra are plotted side by side.
