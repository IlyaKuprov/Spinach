# Heteronuclear trajectory references

The uniform needle has longitudinal field proportional to the sum of source densities; only the observed isotope's own transverse magnetisation contributes to its transverse field. Proton and carbon signed phases are compared to this analytic precession at zero and finite carbon source strength. A reflecting-slab cosine mode tests the two independently lifted molecular diffusion rates. A carbon-selective Y pulse followed by an oblique finite gradient checks the signed phase at an off-centre voxel on three unequal physical axes.

A separate passive-reporter check localises the carbon receiver in a decaying proton cosine grating. Its phase integrates the known exponential decay of the source while the reporter remains stationary.
