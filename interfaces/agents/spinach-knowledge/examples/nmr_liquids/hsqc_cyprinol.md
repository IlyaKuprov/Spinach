# examples/nmr_liquids/hsqc_cyprinol.m

- Signature: `hsqc_cyprinol()`

## Purpose

HSQC spectrum of cyprinol with natural abundance of 13C isotope. Calculation time: seconds

## Physical / mathematical content

The script simulates an HSQC spectrum from the cyprinol spin system, with 13C isotopomers generated by dilution. The acquisition specifies a 150 Hz J value and observes 13C and 1H, decoupling 1H in F1 and 13C in F2.

## Numerical / algorithmic content

The basis is IK-1 sphten-liouv with scalar-coupling connectivity, proximity level 1 and interaction level 3. Each isotopomer is simulated in parallel; square-cosine apodisation, F2 then F1 Fourier transforms, and States combination form the spectrum.

## Implementation structure

At 11.7 T, the sequence uses sweeps [12000 2500] Hz, offsets [5000 1250] Hz, 128 points per dimension and 512-point zero filling. The spin system is created from `cyprinol()` with greedy algorithm options (proximity cutoff 4.0, interaction cutoff 5.0); the real spectrum is plotted with positive polarity.
